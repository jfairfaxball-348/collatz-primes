## CP-0047 — PROVED_INTERNAL: infinite strict D<K families for EVERY fixed admissible (a,c)

**Theorem.** Fix ANY integers a,c>=1 with 3^a>2^(a+c). Put κ=(2^c-1)/3^a, so 0<κ<1. Fix ANY real 0<ε<η<κ. For ALL sufficiently large positive integers m with ε<δ_m<η, where j=ceil(m log_2 3), P=3^m and δ_m=(2^j-P)/P, set d=m-a and b=j-m-c. Then d,b>=1, the word W=O^a E^c O^d E^b has its TRUE FIRST coefficient crossing at precisely j, 0<D<K, and for EVERY q>=0 its positive starting integer n_q=2^a(t0+Mq)-1 STRICTLY descends at j. There are infinitely many eligible m and, for each, infinitely many COMPOSITE n_q with this certificate. No claim covers all n in any modulus.

**Proof.** α=log_2 3 is irrational: otherwise 2^u=3^v with positive integers u,v. Consequently {mα} is dense modulo1, and infinitely many m have j-mα in the nonempty interval (log_2(1+ε),log_2(1+η)); for such m the claimed strict bounds on δ_m hold. Since j/m→α>1, both d=m-a and b=j-m-c tend to infinity.

All O inputs up to a give coefficient (3/2)^i>1. Through E^c, the smallest coefficient is 3^a/2^(a+c)>1 by hypothesis. Subsequent O^d steps raise the slope, so at j-b=m+c the coefficient 3^m/2^(m+c)>1 for m sufficiently large. Along the final E^b segment the coefficient decreases, but j=ceil(mα), hence 2^(j-1)<P<2^j and all j'<j in that last segment have 2^j'<=2^(j-1)<P. Thus the coefficient first falls below1 EXACTLY at j.

Compute K/P = (2^c-1)/3^a - 2^(m-a+c)/3^m = κ-2^(c-a)(2/3)^m. For all sufficiently large m it exceeds η, while δ_m<η: D/P=δ_m<K/P, so 0<D<K.

## CP-0048 — PROVED_INTERNAL: the S011 long even-tail union is nonexhaustive (and no noncircular stopping algorithm)

Therefore n=27 belongs to **NONE** of the W_N cylinders, N>=1.
